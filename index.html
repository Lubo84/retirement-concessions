The one-call script

“I have a concession card. Can you check whether I qualify for a concession on this account, whether it's already applied, and what I need to do next?”

Ask when it starts, whether you need to renew it and how it will appear on your next bill. Have your card and latest bill handy when contacting the provider.

A few useful things to know

I don't receive the Age Pension. Is this still useful?

Yes. If you're 67 or older, check the Commonwealth Seniors Health Card. It has an income test and no assets test. You must also meet the other conditions, including not receiving a Centrelink or DVA income support payment. Your state or territory's Seniors Card program may be worth checking too.

Does a large super balance rule me out?

Not automatically for the Commonwealth Seniors Health Card. Its income test considers adjusted taxable income and, where applicable, deemed income from account-based pensions. This is different from simply checking how much you withdraw. Ask Services Australia how your income streams are assessed.

Does every card offer the same concessions?

No. The card type, where you live, your circumstances and the provider's rules all matter. A business discount card may offer different benefits from a Seniors Card. Ask about the exact card you hold before relying on a discount.

Can I get help without applying online?

For Age Pension and the Commonwealth Seniors Health Card, contact Services Australia's Older Australians line on 132 300. For other health care cards, use the Services Australia contact directory. Your state's Seniors Card application page lists its assistance options.

I already have a card. What should I do?

Start at Step 2. Check whether the concession is applied to the account, keep your details current and ask whether you need to reapply after moving or changing providers. Use the savings tracker only for benefits you have confirmed.

What if I receive another government payment or have a veterans' card?

You may already qualify for a different card or pathway. Review Services Australia's card guide or contact DVA about veterans' cards. This finder covers common retirement pathways and does not cover every entitlement.
